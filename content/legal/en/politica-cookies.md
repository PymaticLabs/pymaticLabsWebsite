## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in the browser of whoever visits it. Article 22(2) of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) requires informing about them and, except for technical or strictly necessary ones, asking for consent before using them.

## 2. Which cookies this site uses

**Only technical cookies, and only when you ask for them.** pymaticlabs.com does not set analytics, advertising or social media cookies, its own or third-party. That is why we do not show you a cookie banner.

- **Light or dark theme.** If you change the theme with the sun and moon button, we save your choice in a first-party cookie, `pymatic_theme`, for one year, so the site looks the same next time. It only stores your choice (`light` or `dark`), it is only set when you press the button, and you can delete it from your browser.
- **Customer area session.** If you sign in to the customer area with your email and your code, we keep your session in a first-party cookie, `sb-<project>-auth-token` (if it is long, the browser splits it into several: `.0`, `.1`...). It stores your session data (your email and some signed tokens that expire), not a password: the area has none. It is only set when you sign in, only our server reads it (pages cannot access it) and it is deleted when you sign out or after 7 days without using the area. It is strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for, so it needs no consent: without it you cannot stay signed in to the area.
- **Language.** The language is in the page address (`/en` for English), not in a cookie.
- **Visit measurement.** We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate without cookies or identifiers stored in your browser. The details are in the website privacy policy.

## 3. Third-party sites

Some links take you to third-party services, such as Stripe's payment page and customer portal, or the calendar to book a call. Those sites have their own cookie policies, which apply when you visit them.

## 4. Changes

If this site ever starts using non-technical cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
