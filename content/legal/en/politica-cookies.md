## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in the browser of whoever visits it. Article 22(2) of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) requires informing about them and, except for technical or strictly necessary ones, asking for consent before using them. The same applies to any other data a website stores in your browser, such as local storage.

## 2. Which cookies and storage this site uses

**Only technical ones, and only when you ask for them.** pymaticlabs.com does not set analytics, advertising or social media cookies, its own or third-party, nor does it store anything similar in your browser. On your first visit it stores nothing. That is why we do not show you a cookie banner.

- **Light or dark theme.** If you change the theme with the sun and moon button, we save your choice in a first-party cookie, `pymatic_theme`, for one year, so the site looks the same next time. It only stores your choice (`light` or `dark`), it is only set when you press the button, and you can delete it from your browser.
- **Member area session.** If you sign in to the member area with your email and your code, we keep your session in a first-party cookie, `sb-<project>-auth-token` (if it is long, the browser splits it into several: `.0`, `.1`...). It stores your session data (your email and some signed tokens that expire), not a password: the area has none. It is only set when you sign in, only our server reads it (pages cannot access it) and it is deleted when you sign out or after 7 days without using the area. It is strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for, so it needs no consent: without it you cannot stay signed in to the area.
- **Signed-in hint.** When you sign in to the member area we store `pymatic_socio`, with the value `1`, in your browser's local storage (it is not a cookie), so the top bar shows your profile instead of "Sign in" while it checks your session. It holds no data about you and gives access to nothing (only the session cookie does), and it is removed when you sign out or as soon as the site sees you no longer have a session. It is technical, so it needs no consent.
- **Language.** The language is in the page address (`/en` for English), not in a cookie.
- **Visit measurement.** We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate without cookies or identifiers stored in your browser. The details are in the [website privacy policy](/en/politica-privacidad).

## 3. Third-party sites

Some links take you to third-party services, such as Stripe's payment page and customer portal, or the Cal.com calendar to book a video call. Those sites have their own cookie policies, which apply when you visit them.

## 4. Changes

If this site ever starts using non-technical cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
