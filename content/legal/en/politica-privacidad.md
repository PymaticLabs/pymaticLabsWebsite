## 3. Who receives the data

Providers that process it on behalf of Pymatic Labs, under a data processing agreement:

- **Vercel**, which hosts the website, keeps the technical logs and measures visits.
- **Resend**, which delivers the form messages to our mailbox.
- **Google** (Workspace), where our email is.
- **Supabase**, in its European Union region (Ireland), which provides access to the member area, stores its data and counts the form submissions.
- **Brevo** (Sendinblue SAS, France), which delivers the codes to sign in to the area.
- **Cal.com**, which provides the free slots for the setup sessions on the purchase page.

When you pay you move to **Stripe**'s page, which processes the payment data as explained in the [Digital Brain privacy policy](/en/privacidad-producto) and under its own privacy policy.

If you give a VAT number from another European Union country or from Monaco (or, as tax ID, a number in that format), we check it in **VIES**, the European Commission's system that verifies it with the tax authority that issued it; the query also carries Pymatic Labs' VAT number. If the VAT number is valid, we keep the query number and its date with the order and, if VIES provides them, the name and address it has on record. We keep nothing from the check of a number you give as tax ID: if it is valid, we ask you to enter it as a VAT number.

If you book a video call, you do so in Cal.com's calendar, with its own privacy policy. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 4. International transfers

Brevo processes the data in the European Union. Vercel, Resend, Google and Cal.com may process it in the United States. Supabase stores it in its European Union region (Ireland), but uses a Singapore entity. They only do so with the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR:

- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** (Commission adequacy decision, Article 45 GDPR): Vercel, Google and Cal.com appear as certified and active on the official list ([dataprivacyframework.gov](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov), checked on 25 October 2026). Resend appears as active, with its recertification under review.
- **European Commission standard contractual clauses** (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914; Article 46(2)(c) GDPR), included in the data processing agreement of all of them. They are the main safeguard for Supabase, which is not in the Framework; for the others, Resend included, they back up the Framework where it does not cover the transfer.

If you ask us at [info@pymaticlabs.com](mailto:info@pymaticlabs.com), we will send you a copy of each provider's safeguards.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Contact messages with no later purchase:** as long as needed to deal with your query and at most 12 months from the last contact.
- **If you become a customer:** they are then governed by the Digital Brain privacy policy.
- **Encrypted fingerprints of the IP address and the email** (form, purchase page and member area): until the next day; the one carried by a launch-price payment, for as long as that payment is kept in Stripe.
- **Technical logs and visit measurement:** the period Vercel applies, which is short and not used to identify you.
- **Member area:** codes, minutes, until used or expired; the session, until you sign out or after 7 days without using it; access logs, Supabase's short period. Everything else in the area, as the Digital Brain privacy policy says.

When it is no longer needed, data is blocked while liabilities may arise and then deleted.
